📖 AK Thulla Arena

Objective

Empty your hand. The last player left with cards is the Bhabhi (Thulla) — the loser!

The Power 👑

After each trick, the player who played the highest card of the lead suit holds the Power and leads next.

Playing a Trick

  • Power holder plays any card — sets the lead suit.
  • Others follow clockwise, must match suit if possible.
  • If you can't follow suit — play any card = THULLA!
  • A Thulla ends the trick immediately.
  • All follow suit → cards go to waste pile (no pickup).
  • Thulla played → highest lead-suit card picks up all.

Escaping

  • Empty your hand = safely escaped!
  • If you hold the Power when you empty your hand, draw 1 from waste to lead next trick.
