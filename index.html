📖 AK Thulla Arena — Rules

OBJECTIVE

Empty your hand. The last player left holding cards is the Bhabhi (Thulla) — the loser!

THE POWER 👑

The player who played the highest card of the lead suit in the last trick has the Power and leads the next trick.

PLAYING A TRICK

  • The Power holder plays any card — sets the lead suit.
  • Players follow clockwise, matching suit if possible.
  • If you can't follow suit, play any card — this is a THULLA!
  • A Thulla ends the trick immediately. Next player does NOT play.
  • If everyone follows suit → cards go to waste pile (no pickup!).
  • If a Thulla was played → player with highest lead-suit card picks up ALL.

GETTING OUT

  • Run out of cards = safely escaped!
  • If you finish cards but had the Power, you draw 1 card from waste to lead.

ONLINE PLAY

Create room → share code → friends join → host starts. Supports 4–8 players. Empty seats = AI bots.
