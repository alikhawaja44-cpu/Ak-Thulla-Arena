📖 How to Play Thulla

OBJECTIVE

Empty your hand. Last player with cards = Bhabhi (Thulla) — the loser!

POWER 👑

Highest lead-suit card holder gets Power and leads next trick.

TRICK

  • Power holder leads — sets lead suit
  • Others must follow suit if possible
  • Can't follow? Play anything = THULLA! — ends trick
  • All follow suit → cards go to waste pile
  • Thulla played → highest lead-suit player picks up all

ESCAPING

  • Empty hand = escaped!
  • Hold Power when emptying → draw 1 from waste
