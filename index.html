📖 How to Play Thulla

OBJECTIVE

Empty your hand. The last player left with cards is the Bhabhi (Thulla) — the loser!

THE POWER 👑

The player with the highest lead-suit card gets Power and leads the next trick.

PLAYING A TRICK

  • Power holder leads any card — sets lead suit
  • Others follow clockwise, must match suit if possible
  • Can't follow? Play anything — that's a THULLA!
  • A Thulla ends the trick immediately
  • All follow suit → cards go to waste pile
  • Thulla played → highest lead-suit player picks up all

ESCAPING

  • Empty your hand = escaped safely!
  • If you hold Power when you empty, draw 1 from waste
